# Khatib — Premium Launch

32 seconds • 1920×1080 • 24 fps • H.264 / AAC stereo

Open `Khatib-Premium-Launch.mp4` to watch. Open `Khatib-Premium-Launch.blend` in Blender 5.2 to edit. The default scene is the Video Sequencer master, with editable titles, transitions, sound and timeline markers. Switch the scene selector to the Khatib scenes to inspect their native geometry, materials, camera animation and lighting.

The timeline uses rendered scene clips for responsive playback. After changing a 3D scene, render its five-second clip again and reload the corresponding video strip. Keep this folder alongside `hero-silent.mp4` in its parent folder; the project uses relative media paths. Fonts and chess artwork are packed in Blender.

The victory statements were supplied by the engine's creator. Opponents remain unnamed. The animated boards are conceptual motion graphics, not reconstructions of those games. No win count, Elo or benchmark number was invented. No AI-generated images or HTML rendering are used.
