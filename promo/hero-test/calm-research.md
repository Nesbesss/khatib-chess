# Revision: reduce competing events

The last versions tried to solve an editorial problem by replacing tracks and adding synchronised effects. The opening alone had six shots, text changes, transient sounds and a blackout before repeating the same reveal. The soundtrack's strongest onset was selected numerically; that does not establish musical suitability. The next sound choice needs an audible reference and audition.

## Research

- Ordinary Folk considers sound throughout message, design and animation, then works from an animatic with composers: https://www.ordinaryfolk.co/process
- Sound on Sound discusses selective sound and leaving space for the other soundtrack elements: https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/sound-design-visual-media
- Paul Slemmer's account of Tilt describes creating tactile sound with foley and synths specifically for motion: https://slemmercreative.com/tilt-sound-design
- Ordinary Folk's design interview provides a reference for developing boards before animation: https://schoolofmotion.com/blog/how-to-design-for-animation-with-ordinary-folk

## Application to Khatib

Keep the approved native 3D materials and camera shots. First establish a calm picture edit: five sustained shots, one message per shot, quieter typography, no cut flashes, no repeated opening montage. This is a pacing study, not a final ad or a completed audio revision.

Music should be auditioned against the reveal before scoring the full timeline. Choose one coherent musical phrase with space, a controlled rise, and a natural resolution. Use only a few effects that express a visible change. Do not attach an effect to every edit. A user reference is pending to resolve musical taste after several rejected tracks.
